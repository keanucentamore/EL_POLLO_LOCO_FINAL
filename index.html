How to play

The story

Pepe was enjoying a quiet siesta in the desert when Señora Gallina, the biggest and angriest hen in all of Mexico, sent her flock after him. The only thing she fears is Pepe's famous hot salsa. Run through the desert, collect the coins, grab every bottle of salsa you can find and show Señora Gallina who is the boss!

Controls

Key Action
← → Walk left and right
Space or ↑ Jump
D Throw a salsa bottle

On phones and tablets, use the buttons on the screen.

Tips

Jump on the chickens to defeat them. Touching them from the side hurts.

You can carry up to five bottles. Señora Gallina needs five hits.
